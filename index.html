Why is the Present Simple so important?

We use it every single day to talk about habits, routines, facts, and things that are always true. "I drink coffee every morning." "She works in a hospital." "The sun rises in the east." Master this tense and you can already say SO much in English!

    Negative Sentences 🚫

    How to say what you DON'T do — using don't and doesn't correctly every time.

    ⏱ 35–45 min📌 don't / doesn't🎯 Express what you don't do
    📖 Grammar Focus — Negatives

    Two words to learn: DON'T and DOESN'T

    I / You / We / They + don't + verbHe / She / It + doesn't + verb (no -s on the main verb!)
    DON'T (I/You/We/They)
    I don't eat meat.
    You don't like spiders.
    We don't work on Sundays.
    They don't speak French.
    DOESN'T (He/She/It) ⚠️
    He doesn't eat meat. ✓
    He doesn't eats meat. ✗

    She doesn't like spiders. ✓
    She doesn't likes spiders. ✗
    ⚠️ Critical Rule
    After DOESN'T, the main verb goes back to its BASE form (no -s).
    She studies → She doesn't study (NOT ~~doesn't studies~~)

    Error Correction

    Each sentence has a mistake. Find it and write the correct version.

    1. She don't likes classical music.
    2. He doesn't works in the morning — only in the afternoon.
    3. I doesn't have a pet.
    4. They not eat breakfast — they wake up too late.
    Correct Versions
  • She doesn't like classical music. (she = doesn't; base verb, no -s)
  • He doesn't work in the morning. (doesn't = base verb, no -s)
  • I don't have a pet. (I = don't, not doesn't)
  • They don't eat breakfast. (must use do/don't to make negative)

    Time Expressions & My Routine ✨

    The words that tell you HOW OFTEN and WHEN — plus a final big writing task to put it all together.

    ⏱ 40–50 min📌 Adverbs of frequency🎯 Talk about your life
    📖 Grammar Focus — Frequency & Time

    Where do these words go in the sentence?

    Adverbs of Frequency
    100% → always — I always drink water.
    80% → usually — She usually works late.
    60% → often — We often eat out.
    40% → sometimes — He sometimes runs.
    20% → rarely / hardly ever
    0% → never — They never watch TV.

    ⚠️ Position: BEFORE the main verb.
    I always eat breakfast. ✓
    I eat always breakfast. ✗
    Time Expressions
    every day / week / month / year
    on Mondays / on weekdays / on weekends
    in the morning / afternoon / evening
    at night / at 7am / at lunchtime
    once / twice / three times a week
    in the morning / at the weekend

    ⚠️ Position: usually at the END of the sentence.
    I go to the gym on Mondays. ✓
